# World Map

> Project: WHERE THE SOUNDINGS END  
> Status: Working baseline  
> Owner: Project lead  
> Version: 0.1  
> Last reviewed: 2026-11-10

The six-region structure is a planning scaffold, not final geometry. Greyhaven is the hub; The Shallows teach basics; Blackrock Reach introduces exposed waters; The Drowned Parish carries historical evidence; The Outer Shoals test long-range navigation; The Hollow Trench anchors the endgame. Design around travel time, landmarks, fuel range, weather exposure, alternate routes, safe returns, and discovery density. Exact distances require a prototype.
