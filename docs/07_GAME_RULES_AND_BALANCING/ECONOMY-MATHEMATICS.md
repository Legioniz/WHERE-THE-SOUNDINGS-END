Expected trip profit = catch sale + contracts + discovery rewards - fuel - bait - repairs - fees - expected loss. Prices can combine base species value with bounded size, quality, condition, and local-demand modifiers. Each upgrade needs cost, capability gained, route/activity unlocked, expected trips to repay, and earlier-equipment relevance. Simulate new, average, and optimized play styles to identify dominant farming loops.
