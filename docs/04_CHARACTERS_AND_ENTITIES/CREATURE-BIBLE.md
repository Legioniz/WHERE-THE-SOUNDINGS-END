# Creature Bible

> Project: WHERE THE SOUNDINGS END  
> Status: Working baseline  
> Owner: Project lead  
> Version: 0.1  
> Last reviewed: 2026-11-10

Threat archetypes: observers, territorial entities, ambush predators, mimics, and deep-water entities. Planning target: 6–8 major threats. Each creature needs habitat, sensory model, movement envelope, states, escalation cues, counterplay, audio signature, cooldowns, and readable tells. Fear must not rely on arbitrary behavior.
