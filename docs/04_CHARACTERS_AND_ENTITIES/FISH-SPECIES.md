# Fish Species

> Project: WHERE THE SOUNDINGS END  
> Status: Working baseline  
> Owner: Project lead  
> Version: 0.1  
> Last reviewed: 2026-11-10

Planning target: 45–60 ordinary species and 10–15 anomalous species. Each data record needs stable ID, name, region, depth band, temperature range, preferred current, time/weather affinity, migration, bait/gear compatibility, size distribution, abundance, market value, catch difficulty, and ecological role. Fictional data must not be presented as real biological fact.
