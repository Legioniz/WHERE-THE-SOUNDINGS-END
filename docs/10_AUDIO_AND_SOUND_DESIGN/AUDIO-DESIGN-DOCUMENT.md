# Audio Design Document

> Project: WHERE THE SOUNDINGS END  
> Status: Working baseline  
> Owner: Project lead  
> Version: 0.1  
> Last reviewed: 2026-11-10

Audio identifies location, direction, distance, machinery state, weather, fish activity, and possible threats. Hierarchy: safety/navigation cues, interaction feedback, ambient bed, threat cues, narrative signals, music. Do not make essential information audio-only; provide visual/subtitle equivalents and accessibility alternatives for directional cues.
