# Art Direction

> Project: WHERE THE SOUNDINGS END  
> Status: Working baseline  
> Owner: Project lead  
> Version: 0.1  
> Last reviewed: 2026-11-10

Restrained, weathered, maritime, grounded, legible. Controlled 2.5D composition with a 3D world and camera prioritizing silhouettes, landmarks, vessel readability, and water scale. Horror intensifies through lighting, negative space, distortion, and environmental change rather than constant visual noise. Define a style sheet before producing asset volume; avoid generic asset-store collage.
