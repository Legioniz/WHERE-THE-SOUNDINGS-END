# Game Scope

> Project: WHERE THE SOUNDINGS END  
> Status: Working baseline  
> Owner: Project lead  
> Version: 0.1  
> Last reviewed: 2026-11-10

## MVP
One compact region; safe harbour and hazardous area; boat control/collision/fuel/damage; five fish; one fishing interaction; cargo, sale, fuel and repairs; two NPCs; one quest; two weather states; fog; minimal Sounding Rig with normal/anomalous signal; one readable threat; save/load; failure recovery; 15–30 minutes of play.

## Vertical slice
A polished 45–90 minute segment proving boat feel, fishing, navigation, audio, Sounding Rig, one threat, narrative, save reliability, and target-PC performance. Do not expand to all regions before this gate.

## Full-game targets
Six regions; 45–60 ordinary fish; 10–15 anomalous fish; 12–15 major NPCs; 25–35 main quests; 30–45 side quests; 35–50 locations; 6–8 threats; 20–30 upgrades; three endings.

No multiplayer, infinite procedural ocean, full naval combat, crew management, full fluid simulation, or default permadeath in initial scope. New major features require a scope trade-off.
