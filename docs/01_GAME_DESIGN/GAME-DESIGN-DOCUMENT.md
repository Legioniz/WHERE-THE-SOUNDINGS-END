# Game Design Document

> Project: WHERE THE SOUNDINGS END  
> Status: Working baseline  
> Owner: Project lead  
> Version: 0.2  
> Last reviewed: 2026-11-12

## High concept

A working mariner returns to an isolated island village and takes a job on an old fishing boat. Three boats have gone beyond the outer marker and only one has returned. The sea is not simply dangerous: its sounds and returns sometimes contradict what the player can measure, and the protagonist recognises a pattern from a survey vessel lost eight years earlier.

**Genre:** 2.5D open-world fishing survival horror. **Mode:** single-player. **Platform:** Windows PC first. **Horror:** systemic, intense, atmosphere- and audio-led. **Playtime goal:** 20+ hours, subject to vertical-slice validation.

## Player fantasy

The player is a capable but vulnerable small-boat mariner. They learn the water by reading weather, currents, landmarks, catches, machinery and sound. Knowledge and preparation matter as much as money and equipment.

## Core loop

Village preparation → choose destination and gear → depart → navigate and read conditions → fish, investigate or sound the seabed → respond to risk → return or continue → sell, repair, rest, interpret discoveries → update world and story state.

## Differentiators

1. **Sounding Rig:** deployable acoustic receiver that reveals clues imperfectly; signals may be false, distorted or reactive.
2. **Living Current:** regional fish availability responds to habitat, time, weather, migration and fishing pressure.
3. **Acoustic Navigation:** bells, buoys, waves, engines, radio and echoes orient the player when visibility fails.
4. **Memory of the Sea:** extraction, investigation and disturbance leave persistent consequences.
5. **Salvage Ledger:** failure imposes recovery costs and possible cargo loss without erasing long-term progress.

## World and story

The six working regions are Greyhaven, The Shallows, Blackrock Reach, The Drowned Parish, The Outer Shoals and The Hollow Trench.

The proposed main story has four acts:
1. **Familiar Waters** — establish Greyhaven, routine, missing boats and the first verified acoustic contradiction.
2. **Beyond the Coast** — expand the investigation and discover that related incidents predate the Orison.
3. **The Deep** — connect the Drowned Parish, the Orison's course diversion and Nell's final warning.
4. **The Last Sounding** — reach the final acoustic boundary and choose how to respond.

The proposed central mystery is called **the Choir**: a recurring pattern of acoustic anomalies, not a confirmed creature or fully explained intelligence. The protagonist was an apprentice sounder aboard the lost vessel Orison; their sister Nell was its radio operator and their father its captain. The protagonist was the only person recovered.

Three core endings are proposed: **The Answer**, **The Silence** and **The Last Mark**. The Last Mark is the recommended thematic ending; the optional Keeper epilogue is deferred. See the narrative documents for full evidence and choice requirements. This proposal is not yet implemented or approved as final canon.

## Planning targets

45–60 ordinary fish; 10–15 anomalous species; 12–15 major NPCs plus minor population; 25–35 main quests; 30–45 side quests; 35–50 locations; 6–8 major threats; 20–30 upgrades. These are planning ceilings, not guaranteed launch content.
