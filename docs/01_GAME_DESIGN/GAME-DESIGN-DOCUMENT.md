# Game Design Document

> Project: WHERE THE SOUNDINGS END  
> Status: Working baseline  
> Owner: Project lead  
> Version: 0.1  
> Last reviewed: 2026-11-10

## High concept
A working fisherman in an isolated island village inherits a routine shadowed by a missing fisherman's disappearance. The sea is not simply dangerous: it listens, remembers, and changes in response to what people take from it.

**Genre:** 2.5D open-world fishing survival horror. **Mode:** single-player. **Platform:** Windows PC first. **Horror:** intense, systemic, audio- and atmosphere-led. **Playtime goal:** 20+ hours, subject to vertical-slice validation.

## Player fantasy
The player is a capable but vulnerable small-boat fisherman. They learn the water by reading weather, currents, landmarks, catches, machinery, and sound. Knowledge and preparation matter as much as money and equipment.

## Core loop
Village preparation → choose destination and gear → depart → navigate and read conditions → fish, investigate, or sound the seabed → respond to risk → return or continue → sell, repair, rest, interpret discoveries → update world and story state.

## Differentiators
1. Sounding Rig: deployable acoustic receiver that reveals clues imperfectly; signals may be false, distorted, or reactive.
2. Living Current: regional fish availability responds to habitat, time, weather, migration, and fishing pressure.
3. Acoustic Navigation: bells, buoys, waves, engines, radio, and echoes orient the player when visibility fails.
4. Memory of the Sea: extraction, investigation, and disturbance leave persistent consequences.
5. Salvage Ledger: failure imposes recovery costs and possible cargo loss without erasing long-term progress.

## World and story
Greyhaven, The Shallows, Blackrock Reach, The Drowned Parish, The Outer Shoals, and The Hollow Trench. Four acts: Familiar Waters, Beyond the Coast, The Deep, The Last Sounding. Three planned endings: The Return, The Silence, The Descent. Exact geography, lore, plot events, and endings remain subject to canon approval.

## Planning targets
45–60 ordinary fish; 10–15 anomalous species; 12–15 major NPCs plus minor population; 25–35 main quests; 30–45 side quests; 35–50 locations; 6–8 major threats; 20–30 upgrades. These are planning ceilings, not guaranteed launch content.
