# Game Pillars

> Project: WHERE THE SOUNDINGS END  
> Status: Working baseline  
> Owner: Project lead  
> Version: 0.1  
> Last reviewed: 2026-11-10

1. The sea is a system: ecology, sound, weather, light, and threats influence one another.
2. Knowledge is equipment: charts, interpretation, testimony, and observation improve survival.
3. Fishing is the livelihood: it must be satisfying, legible, and economically meaningful.
4. Fear is earned: threats follow learnable rules, even when their identity is uncertain.
5. Consequences preserve commitment: failure hurts without arbitrarily erasing hours.
6. Atmosphere is interactive: sound, fog, light, and environmental reactions carry information.
