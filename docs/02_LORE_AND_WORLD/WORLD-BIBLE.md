# World Bible

> Project: WHERE THE SOUNDINGS END  
> Status: Working baseline  
> Owner: Project lead  
> Version: 0.2  
> Last reviewed: 2026-11-10

An isolated island community depends on surrounding fishing waters. A fisherman has disappeared. The protagonist takes up work connected to that absence and discovers signs of an older, poorly understood relationship between village and sea. The ocean is a connected system of ecology, physical spaces, memories, acoustic signals, and entities; extraction and disturbance can change what is encountered.

## Regions
- Greyhaven: home village, harbour, service hub, opening routine.
- The Shallows: accessible fishing grounds and learning space.
- Blackrock Reach: exposed waters and geological hazards.
- The Drowned Parish: submerged settlement remains and historical evidence.
- The Outer Shoals: remote migratory grounds and difficult navigation.
- The Hollow Trench: deep-water endgame region.

## Geography and ecological baseline

The working geological model is a volcanic island arc with a sheltered bay, shallow island shelf, exposed volcanic coastline, drowned river valley, offshore ridges, submarine canyons, and a deep tectonic margin. This is a design foundation, not a locked real-world coordinate or final tectonic reconstruction.

The provisional ecological model includes estuary and mangrove-edge habitats, seagrass meadows, coral and rocky reefs, intertidal shelves, pelagic migration corridors, seamount-like hardgrounds, deep-sea canyon communities, cold seeps, hydrothermal vents, and occasional large food falls. Climate and exact regional species composition remain unapproved.

Wildlife is not synonymous with hostility. Most animals feed, migrate, rest, shelter, reproduce, scavenge, or avoid the boat. Player-directed attacks require an authored ecological or anomalous reason. The regional distribution, local fauna, difficult sublocations, and environmental activity rules are documented in [Regional Ecology and Creature Distribution](../05_WORLD_AND_LEVEL_DESIGN/REGIONAL-ECOLOGY-AND-CREATURES.md). Unnatural presences are catalogued separately in [Anomalous Species](../04_CHARACTERS_AND_ENTITIES/ANOMALOUS-SPECIES.md).

## Provisional facts

Fishermen have disappeared or returned under unexplained circumstances. The lighthouse keeper reports seeing things in illuminated water that cannot be seen clearly from shore. The Sounding Rig supplies evidence, never omniscient answers. Village beliefs conflict.
