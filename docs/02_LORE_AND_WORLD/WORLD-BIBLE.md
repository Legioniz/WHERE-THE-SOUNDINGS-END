# World Bible

> Project: WHERE THE SOUNDINGS END  
> Status: Working baseline  
> Owner: Project lead  
> Version: 0.1  
> Last reviewed: 2026-11-10

An isolated island community depends on surrounding fishing waters. A fisherman has disappeared. The protagonist takes up work connected to that absence and discovers signs of an older, poorly understood relationship between village and sea. The ocean is a connected system of ecology, physical spaces, memories, acoustic signals, and entities; extraction and disturbance can change what is encountered.

## Regions
- Greyhaven: home village, harbour, service hub, opening routine.
- The Shallows: accessible fishing grounds and learning space.
- Blackrock Reach: exposed waters and geological hazards.
- The Drowned Parish: submerged settlement remains and historical evidence.
- The Outer Shoals: remote migratory grounds and difficult navigation.
- The Hollow Trench: deep-water endgame region.

## Provisional facts
Fishermen have disappeared or returned under unexplained circumstances. The lighthouse keeper reports seeing things in illuminated water that cannot be seen clearly from shore. The Sounding Rig supplies evidence, never omniscient answers. Village beliefs conflict.

## Canon not yet locked
Exact geography, chronology, supernatural ontology, the cause of disappearances, protagonist identity, missing fisherman's identity, and ending truth. Mark speculation until approved.
