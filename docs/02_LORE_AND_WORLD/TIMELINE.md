# Narrative Timeline

> Project: WHERE THE SOUNDINGS END  
> Status: Proposed relative chronology — fixed story intervals, broad older history  
> Owner: Project lead  
> Version: 0.2  
> Last reviewed: 2026-11-14

## Dating convention

The current story opening is **Year 0**. Dates are relative to the opening until a named in-world calendar is approved. Older settlement history is deliberately described by generations rather than false precision.

The two fixed intervals are:
- the lighthouse bell's last confirmed sounding is **11 years before Year 0**;
- the Orison is lost **8 years before Year 0**.

The bell therefore fell silent three years before the Orison incident.

## Chronology checks

1. The lighthouse bell's silence is older than the Orison loss by exactly three years in the current proposal.
2. The Orison is lost eight years before the present opening.
3. The current three-boat incident is not the same incident as the Orison loss.
4. The Drowned Parish's submerged bell and the lighthouse bell are separate physical objects unless new evidence establishes a connection.
5. The Choir label is used by people comparing records; it is not a name the phenomenon gives itself.
6. The present four Greyhaven observations do not automatically date back to the Orison.
7. No record may contain knowledge of events that occurred after its author made it.

## Open calendar decisions

The local year length, month names, season names, tide tables, exact dates of the Orison loss, and the age of the Drowned Parish remain undecided. Keep story-critical intervals relative until these are intentionally locked.
