## Editorial rules

1. Distinguish archaeological evidence, historical testimony, mythology, oral tradition, modern folklore, disputed claims, and natural phenomena.
2. Attribute traditions to the specific community or region named by reliable sources. Do not flatten distinct peoples into a single generic culture.
3. Do not present Atlantis, sea monsters, phantom islands, or supernatural explanations as established historical or scientific fact.
4. Do not turn living or sacred cultural figures into collectible monsters, loot piñatas, or boss fights without appropriate consultation and permission.
5. Build original fictional characters, places, rituals, and outcomes. Use a motif as a starting point, not a license to reproduce a tradition wholesale.
6. Keep all research-inspired quests **Proposed / Non-canon** until reviewed against the World Bible and approved.

## Relationship to canon

The [World Bible](WORLD-BIBLE.md) remains the single source of truth for the fictional setting. No entry in this research collection changes Greyhaven, its history, the six regions, or the ocean's supernatural rules by itself.
