# The Price in the Pool

> Project: WHERE THE SOUNDINGS END  
> Status: Proposed / Non-canon  
> Version: 0.1  
> Type: Dialogue mystery / moral bargain  
> Inspiration: Water-spirit bargain stories; not a depiction of Mami Wata

## Short lore

> “A coin appeared on the tide pool stone each morning. No one admitted leaving it. The fishmonger took one, and the next day his nets were full. By the end of the week, every net in the village was full—and no one could remember seeing a fish beneath the water.”

A tide pool near an abandoned landing contains coins that do not match the village currency. People who take one report short-term good fortune, followed by an unusual absence of fish.

## Player hook

The fishmonger asks the player to investigate why the market has received an impossible volume of one species while other catches have vanished from nearby grounds.

## Player activity

1. Inspect the pool and collect evidence without taking the coin.
2. Compare recent market records with the Living Current's local abundance.
3. Ask three NPCs about the coins and record conflicting explanations.
4. Decide whether to return the coins, leave them untouched, or take one as evidence.
5. Monitor the fishing ground over the next few trips.

## Outcome

- **Return the coin:** The pool remains a mystery; local fish availability gradually returns to baseline.
- **Keep the coin:** The player gains a one-time payout, but the fishmonger temporarily loses access to the affected stock.
- **Leave it:** No immediate gain or penalty; a later quest can revisit the pool.

## Reward

A modest payment, a market record, and a world-state change in local fish availability. Avoid an infinite wealth exploit or guaranteed rare fish.

## Dependencies

- Fishing economy and local stock data.
- NPC dialogue conditions.
- Persistent quest flag.
- Time-delayed regional state change.

## Research and originality note

The first report referenced Mami Wata traditions. These traditions are diverse across West and Central African and diasporic contexts; they should not be reduced to a single seductive sea monster or a universal bargain story. This quest uses only the broad fictional motif of a mysterious water-side bargain and does not name or depict Mami Wata.

Reference starting point: https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Mami_Wata  
This is a starting index only; consult specialist scholarship and community-informed sources before any direct representation.

## Scope note

Implement with a short dialogue tree, one tide-pool prop, a local stock modifier, and a clear timed reset. No supernatural NPC model is required.
