## Shared quest-file rules

Every file must state:
- **Status:** Proposed / Non-canon until approved.
- **Player hook:** Why the player cares and how the quest is found.
- **Short lore:** In-world text in original wording.
- **Activity:** What the player actually does.
- **Outcome:** Reward, cost, consequence, or changed world state.
- **Dependencies:** Existing mechanics, regions, NPCs, and content needed.
- **Research note:** Which broad tradition or phenomenon inspired it and what needs further verification.
- **Scope note:** A feasible first implementation, with no hidden requirement for a new major subsystem.

## Review gates

1. Read [Maritime Lore Research Notes](../../../02_LORE_AND_WORLD/MARITIME-LORE-RESEARCH-NOTES.md).
2. Check the [World Bible](../../../02_LORE_AND_WORLD/WORLD-BIBLE.md).
3. Verify sources and specific attribution in the [source register](../../../02_LORE_AND_WORLD/MARITIME-LORE-SOURCES.md).
4. Check the relevant gameplay specs and add testable triggers.
5. Obtain cultural review where a living tradition or sacred figure could be implicated.
6. Approve, revise, or archive the concept before calling it canon.

These twelve files are a first-pass set based on the report's six expanded examples and twelve suggested filenames. They are not twelve fully researched historical case studies.
