# Act IV — The Last Sounding

> Project: WHERE THE SOUNDINGS END  
> Status: Proposed  
> Owner: Project lead  
> Version: 0.2  
> Last reviewed: 2026-11-12

## Dramatic purpose

Bring the player to the place where reliable soundings stop and force a decision that expresses the protagonist's growth. The finale should be tense, navigational and emotionally direct. It should not become a conventional boss fight or deliver a definitive scientific explanation.

**Dramatic question:** When the sea offers the voice the protagonist most wants to hear, will they follow it, silence the response or preserve the evidence and leave a warning?

## Region focus

The final expedition approaches the deepest known acoustic boundary in the Hollow Trench. The player should prepare in Greyhaven, review equipment and evidence, choose a route and understand the risk before departure.

## Sequence

### 1. Preparation and review

- The journal presents the strongest evidence families collected by the player.
- Residents who have earned trust may provide practical support, warnings or a final account.
- The player checks hull condition, engine condition, fuel/operational supplies if those systems exist by then, and the sounding/radio equipment.
- The game makes the final objective clear without revealing every possible interpretation.

### 2. The final survey

At the boundary, the Sounding Rig returns a depth response, then a delayed or contradictory response. Further pings become less reliable. Eventually the expected seabed return does not arrive.

The scene should use environmental subtraction rather than constant escalation: reduced wave sound, missing radio noise, a stable horizon, subtle instrument movement and the engine becoming the only reliable rhythm. A voice resembling Nell may appear, but it must not provide new objective information unavailable elsewhere.

### 3. The final message

If the player has found the relevant evidence, the last transmission can be understood in its correct context:

**"Don't follow the sound. Mark the water."**

The line is a warning and an instruction to preserve a location, not an invitation to pursue a voice. The player should recognise the difference through evidence collected across earlier acts.

### 4. Final action

The player makes one explicit action:
- follow the voice beyond the last reliable return;
- stop answering and cut the active transmission chain;
- record the coordinates, mark the boundary and preserve the evidence before disengaging.

The third route requires the strongest evidence chain and produces the recommended ending. The action should be presented as a choice, not a hidden result of a numerical morality meter.

## Ending aftermath

The endings should return to Greyhaven in different ways. Show the changed routines, the condition of the harbour, how records are kept and whether future mariners receive a warning. Do not rely only on a paragraph of narration.

- The Answer ends with the protagonist lost and a new distress call carrying their voice.
- The Silence ends with the protagonist alive and the immediate signal chain interrupted, but the historical record incomplete.
- The Last Mark ends with the location and names recorded, the active system disabled or abandoned, and the community able to remember the lost without being asked to follow them.
- The optional Keeper epilogue is a separate, lower-priority concept and should only be explored after the three core endings work.

## Final scene constraints

1. No mandatory combat boss.
2. No instant death without clear preceding danger.
3. Do not remove steering control as a hallucination effect.
4. Keep the boat's physical handling and navigation relevant.
5. Preserve the player's journal and evidence in the ending record.
6. Do not confirm Nell's survival or death beyond the evidence the story actually contains.
7. Do not explain the Choir's complete origin.
8. The Last Mark should feel earned through observation and restraint, not a checklist revealed only at the ending.

## Implementation status

Proposed final-act design only. No final trench mission, ending selection, ending scenes or ending-specific save state is implemented.
