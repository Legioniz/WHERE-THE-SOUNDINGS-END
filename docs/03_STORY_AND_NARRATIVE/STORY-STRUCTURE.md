# Story Structure

> Project: WHERE THE SOUNDINGS END  
> Status: Working baseline  
> Owner: Project lead  
> Version: 0.1  
> Last reviewed: 2026-11-10

Act I — Familiar Waters: establish Greyhaven, routine fishing, disappearance, basic systems.
Act II — Beyond the Coast: reveal wider ecological disruption, conflicting testimony, reactive signals.
Act III — The Deep: connect the Drowned Parish, remote grounds, and acoustic disturbance history.
Act IV — The Last Sounding: confront the mystery and make choices shaping the ending.

Each act needs a dramatic question, playable objective, region progression, new mechanic or reinterpretation, reversal, and ending condition. Avoid exposition that replaces play.
