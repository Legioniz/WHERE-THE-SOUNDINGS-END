# Act II — Beyond the Coast

> Project: WHERE THE SOUNDINGS END  
> Status: Proposed  
> Owner: Project lead  
> Version: 0.2  
> Last reviewed: 2026-11-12

## Dramatic purpose

Expand the mystery beyond Greyhaven. Show that different waters produce different evidence and that the player cannot interpret every sound as a threat. Introduce the possibility that the anomaly is geographically distributed or tied to repeated human activity, while preserving ordinary ecological and geological explanations.

**Dramatic question:** Is the signal a local environmental event, a faulty instrument, deliberate human interference or part of a wider pattern?

## Region focus

- **The Shallows:** a baseline for ordinary water-column returns, fishing, reefs and coastal acoustic conditions.
- **Blackrock Reach:** exposed water, basalt coastline, surge and difficult echoes around rock.
- **The Outer Shoals:** remote migratory grounds, deeper water and more complex sounding conditions.

These are narrative destinations; scene availability and travel connections must be verified separately before tasks are assigned.

## Sequence

### 1. Establish a baseline

The player compares the suspicious signal with known conditions:
- a normal sounding in a familiar area;
- a biological scattering-layer return where appropriate;
- a return affected by heading, sea state or engine noise;
- a physical structure that creates an ordinary echo.

The Sounding Rig should be useful but not omniscient. A confidence estimate and repeated-ping procedure should make uncertainty readable.

### 2. A second location

In Blackrock Reach or a suitable Outer Shoals site, the player encounters a related but not identical return. The sound has the same timing signature or repeated interval, but the physical setting is different. The player should be able to document both similarities and contradictions.

A dangerous maritime incident may occur, but it must have authored warning, counterplay and an escape route. The anomaly itself should not automatically become a monster attack.

### 3. Historical discrepancy

A survey chart, maintenance note or recovered log references a comparable return before the Orison disappeared. The date predates the protagonist's personal incident. This changes the central question: the Orison did not create the phenomenon.

A record should also establish that the lighthouse bell was taken out of service or silenced eleven years before the present opening. The exact maintenance cause remains a separate human/historical question until supported by evidence.

### 4. The sea becomes quiet

A restrained sequence removes or reduces several ambient layers: birds, wave texture, distant engines or radio noise. It should begin with a plausible environmental lull and then last long enough for the player to notice the missing sound.

The player retains full control and can use instruments or a known landmark to verify their position. Do not distort input or conceal a real threat warning.

## Act II reversal

The evidence establishes that the acoustic pattern predates the Orison and has been recorded at more than one location. The protagonist's family tragedy is one part of a longer history, not the origin of the event.

## Act II required outcomes

- Player has compared ordinary and anomalous sounding returns.
- Player has recorded at least one independent site with a related acoustic signature.
- Player has found a credible record dated before the Orison incident.
- Player understands that the signal cannot be explained by the current missing-boat incident alone.
- The story has presented at least one non-hostile or ambiguous encounter.

## Optional content

- A rare, location-bound legendary sighting that has its own ecology and rules.
- A damaged net, unusual catch or drifting object that can be explained without the Choir.
- A second witness account that contradicts the first.
- A navigational challenge where the player can choose to retreat rather than continue.

## Exit condition

The player has a concrete lead toward the Drowned Parish and the older history of the bell, soundings and vanished vessels.

## Implementation status

Proposed narrative content only. Regional survey profile and acoustic foundations exist in source, but this act's travel, locations, records and quests are not thereby implemented.
