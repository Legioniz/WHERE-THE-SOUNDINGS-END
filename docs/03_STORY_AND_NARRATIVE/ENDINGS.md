# Endings

> Project: WHERE THE SOUNDINGS END  
> Status: Working baseline  
> Owner: Project lead  
> Version: 0.1  
> Last reviewed: 2026-11-10

Three ending philosophies are reserved: The Return, The Silence, and The Descent. These are thematic directions, not finalized plot summaries. Endings should emerge from understandable choices and accumulated evidence, not an unexplained morality meter. Each needs eligibility flags, final choice, world consequences, unresolved mysteries, and post-ending state.
