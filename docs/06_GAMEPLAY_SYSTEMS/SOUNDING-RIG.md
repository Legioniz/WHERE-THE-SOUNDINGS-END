# Sounding Rig

> Project: WHERE THE SOUNDINGS END  
> Status: Working baseline  
> Owner: Project lead  
> Version: 0.1  
> Last reviewed: 2026-11-10

The signature device is a deployable weighted acoustic receiver/buoy. It samples return delay, amplitude, frequency bands, pattern stability, and noise. It may suggest depth bands, cavities, wreckage, schools, current boundaries, or anomalous sources; it never supplies perfect labels or exact coordinates. Deployment costs time and attention. Some signals may be distorted or reactive. Interference must be learnable rather than arbitrary.
