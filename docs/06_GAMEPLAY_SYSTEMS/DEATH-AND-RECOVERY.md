> Project: WHERE THE SOUNDINGS END  
> Status: Working baseline  
> Owner: Project lead  
> Version: 0.1  
> Last reviewed: 2026-11-10

Use a Salvage Ledger. Record incident, location, cargo/equipment at risk, recovery debt/cost, and story/ecological consequence. Preserve major discoveries and long-term progression by default. Selected cargo or temporary resources may be lost when clearly communicated. Do not routinely erase dozens of hours.
