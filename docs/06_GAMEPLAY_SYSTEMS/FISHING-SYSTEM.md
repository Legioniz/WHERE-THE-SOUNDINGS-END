# Fishing System

> Project: WHERE THE SOUNDINGS END  
> Status: Working baseline  
> Owner: Project lead  
> Version: 0.1  
> Last reviewed: 2026-11-10

A fishing attempt comprises location/equipment selection, bait or method, cast/deployment, wait/feedback, hook/collection interaction, catch resolution, identification, cargo storage, and potential world-state impact. Catch chance depends on eligibility, local stock, habitat, gear, bait, time, weather, and player action. Keep factors interpretable and bounded. Catch interaction should reward readable input, not be a reflex test for every species.
