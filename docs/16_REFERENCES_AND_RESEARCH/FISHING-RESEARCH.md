Relevant ideas include stock abundance, biomass, recruitment, mortality, catchability, gear selectivity, effort, habitat covariates, and CPUE. NOAA's 2024 CPUE good-practice review emphasizes standardization and the influence of gear, targeting, environment, and analytical choices. Use this to inform a simplified fictional ecology, not a scientific stock assessment.
