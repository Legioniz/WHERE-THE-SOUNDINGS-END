## Isolation, sleep, and submarine work
- Chinoy et al., Applied Ergonomics (2024), sleep, fatigue, and lifestyle behaviours aboard submarines: https://pubmed.ncbi.nlm.nih.gov/38820921/
- Van Puyvelde et al., Journal of Applied Physiology (2022), sleep and circadian hormones during a 67-day submarine mission: https://doi.org/10.1152/japplphysiol.00130.2021

These studies support fatigue, sleep, mood, and circadian discussion in their studied submarine contexts. They do not prove anecdotal hallucination stories or establish identical effects in a lone fishing-vessel operator.
